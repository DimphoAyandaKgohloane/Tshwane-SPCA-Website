# Tshwane-SPCA-Website

**Module:** Introduction to Web Development (WEDE5020)
**Student Number:** ST10527228

## Project Overview

A functional, visually appealing website for Tshwane SPCA, a non-profit
animal welfare organisation founded in 1910, operating under the SPCA Act of
1993. It covers approximately 6 200 km&sup2; across Gauteng from branches in
Waltloo and Centurion, responding to cruelty reports 24 hours a day.

## Website Goals and Objectives

- Increase online donations.
- Grow the volunteer base.
- Promote pet adoptions.

## Key Features and Functionality

- **Home** (`index.html`) - mission introduction and urgent needs, with
  donation, adoption and volunteering calls to action placed near the top.
- **About** (`about.html`) - history, mission, service area, target audience.
- **Services** (`services.html`) - inspectorate, adoption, sterilisation and
  veterinary services.
- **Enquiry** (`enquiry.html`) - form allowing visitors to enquire about
  volunteering, becoming a sponsor, donating, or adoption.
- **Contact** (`contact.html`) - both branches (Waltloo and Centurion) with
  map placeholders and a general contact form.

Design: blues and greens to show trust and care, avoiding aggressive
imagery. Donation and adoption placed near the top of the homepage, with
clear, accessible navigation for visitors of all ages.
